# Trail Mix: Scene-by-Scene Outline

## How to read this outline

This outline breaks the story in docs/story.md into **23 scenes**, in the order of *A Walk in the Woods*:
- an opening;
- 13 decision events;
- 5 quiet beats;
- a final choice;
- 4 endings.

If this file and the story doc disagree, this file wins on story details. Each scene lists:
- **Purpose**
- **Book** (chapters)
- **What happens**
- **Choices → consequences**
- **Arcs & themes**

**Stats:** Stamina, Supplies, Bond and Morale run 0–10, and Miles is a counter. They start at Stamina 6, Supplies 6, Bond 5, Morale 6.

**Ending checks:** after E6, after E8, after E11, after E13, and at the Final Crossroads.

**Flags:**
- **skipped ahead** (E6)
- **invited** (E8)
- **Katz away** (E8 to E11)
- **left the wilderness** (E13)

**Scope target:** each scene is about one screen of narration and dialogue plus one choice menu. A run takes about 20–25 minutes.

**Faithfulness:**
- The book's own choice is always one of the options, and is marked *(book)* below.
- Lines paraphrase the book; there are no long quotes.
- **(verify)** marks a fact to check against the book.

## Prologue: Hanover, New Hampshire (winter)

### Scene 1: Opening
- **Purpose:** establish Bryson's voice, the premise and the gap between plan and reality.
- **Book:** Ch 1.
- **What happens:** five illustrated panels with narration:
  1. Bryson moves back to America and finds the trail at the edge of Hanover.
  2. He announces he will walk all of it.
  3. He reads bear-attack books late into the night.
  4. Everyone he invites declines.
  5. Katz calls from Des Moines: he's in.
- **Choices:** none. There is no name entry: the player is Bill Bryson.
- **Arcs & themes:** *the plan* at its most confident and most anxious.

### Scene 2: E1, The Outfitter (tutorial)
- **Purpose:** teach the choice menu and the stats in a funny, low-stakes setting.
- **Book:** Ch 1.
- **What happens:** a salesman walks Bryson round the outfitter, and the pile of gear grows (verify the salesman's name).
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Buy the whole list: Supplies +2, Stamina −1.
  - Buy only what the guidebook says *(book, roughly; verify)*: Stamina +1, Supplies −1.
  - Leave room for whatever Katz brings: Bond +1, Morale +1, Supplies −1.
- **Arcs & themes:** preparation as Bryson's belief system.

## Act I: Georgia (March)

### Scene 3: E2, Katz Lightens His Load
- **Purpose:** introduce Katz properly and set up the first relationship choice.
- **Book:** Ch 2.
- **What happens:** Atlanta. Katz is heavy, cheerful, unready and sober; he says so plainly. Then come Amicalola Falls and the brutal approach trail. Furious with his pack, Katz flings food and gear into the woods.
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Climb down and fetch it: Supplies +1, Stamina −2.
  - Share your food with him: Bond +2, Supplies −2.
  - Laugh it off and keep walking *(book, roughly)*: Morale +1, Bond −1.
- **Arcs & themes:** Katz's arc begins. Humor as coping.

### Scene 4: E3, Mary Ellen
- **Purpose:** comic relief, and a choice made *with* Katz rather than about him.
- **Book:** Ch 5.
- **What happens:** Mary Ellen from Florida joins them uninvited and won't stop talking.
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Let her tag along: Supplies +1, Morale −2.
  - Slip away at dawn with Katz *(book)*: Bond +1, Stamina −1.
  - Tell her you'd rather hike alone: Morale +1, Bond −1.
- **Arcs & themes:** the partnership as a team of two.

### Scene 5: Act I close, the woods at night
- **Purpose:** a quiet beat of wonder, and the first crack in the planner.
- **Book:** Ch 4.
- **What happens:** the dark, the silence, the stars. Bryson realizes the woods are not like anywhere else.
- **Choices:** none. The narration changes with Bond (5+, or 4 or less).

## Act II: The Smokies and Shenandoah (spring)

### Scene 6: E4, Snowstorm
- **Purpose:** the first danger from nature.
- **Book:** Ch 6 (verify where the snow hits).
- **What happens:** a spring snowstorm traps them at a crowded shelter full of other hikers.
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Wait it out: Stamina +1, Morale −1.
  - Push on through the snow: Miles +, Stamina −2.
  - Get down to Gatlinburg early: Supplies +1, Miles −.
- **Arcs & themes:** weather doesn't care about plans.

### Scene 7: E5, Night Noises
- **Purpose:** Bryson's bear dread pays off as comedy, plus a small trust test.
- **Book:** Ch 1 and 6.
- **What happens:** something large moves outside the tent. **It is never identified, whatever you choose.**
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Hang the food bag: Stamina −1, Morale +1.
  - Wake Katz *(book, roughly; verify the knife)*: Bond +1, Stamina −1.
  - Lie still and listen: Morale −2.
- **Arcs & themes:** fear and humor. The wilderness is real, and so is the imagination.

### Scene 8: E6, The Rental Car
- **Purpose:** the book's biggest turn, and the first ending check.
- **Book:** Ch 7–8.
- **What happens:** Gatlinburg. Snow has closed the high trail, and Bill and Katz have barely spoken in two days. They talk about skipping ahead.
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Rent a car and drive north to Virginia *(book)*: Morale +1, Supplies −1. Sets **skipped ahead**.
  - Walk on through, whatever it takes: Stamina −2, Bond −1.
  - Take zero days in Gatlinburg: Stamina +2, Supplies −1, Morale −1.
- **Ending check** follows.
- **Arcs & themes:** *what counts as "really" hiking the trail?* The book's answer begins here.

### Scene 9: E7, Skyline Drive
- **Purpose:** show the tamed, managed wilderness, and the temptation of comfort.
- **Book:** Ch 8, 10.
- **What happens:** in Shenandoah the trail keeps crossing Skyline Drive. There are waysides with burgers, and day-trippers. The Park Service history goes into the Journal.
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Hike every mile: Stamina −1, Morale +1.
  - Stop at every wayside: Bond +1, Morale +1, Supplies −1.
  - Hitch between waysides: Stamina +1, Morale −1.
- **Arcs & themes:** nature vs. the managed park. The second theme (the fragile, badly kept wilderness) comes forward.

### Scene 10: Midpoint, the campfire talk
- **Purpose:** the emotional center. It shows the player what the relationship has become.
- **Book:** Ch 11.
- **What happens:**
  - **Bond 5+:** Katz talks honestly about drinking, getting sober and why he said yes. Bryson tells him he's glad he came.
  - **Bond 4 or less:** Katz has looked up flights home.
- **Choices:** none.
- **Arcs & themes:** the turning point of Katz's arc. Written with care, never as a joke.

### Scene 11: E8, Katz Goes Home
- **Purpose:** close Part 1. Set up whether Katz comes back.
- **Book:** Ch 11 (verify the reason he leaves).
- **What happens:** Katz has to get back to Des Moines. The airport goodbye.
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Ask him to come back for the end: Bond +1, Morale −1. Sets **invited**.
  - Tell him you're glad he came: Bond +2, Morale −2.
  - Drop him at the airport and say little: Morale +1, Bond −2.
- **After the choice:** sets **Katz away**.
- **Ending check** follows.

## Act III: Alone (summer)

### Scene 12: Home in Hanover
- **Purpose:** a breath between halves.
- **Book:** Ch 12.
- **What happens:** a few weeks at home. Recovery, restocking, and the pull of the trail. Shown as one result card with no choice: Stamina +1, Supplies +2.
- **Katz away:** from here until E11, Katz doesn't follow, the HUD shows "Katz: home in Iowa", and Bond can't change.

### Scene 13: E9, Car Hiking
- **Purpose:** the central theme through solitude. Does it count if it's easy?
- **Book:** Ch 12–14.
- **What happens:** Pennsylvania's endless rocks. Bryson walks day sections from his car, and it feels hollow.
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Keep day-hiking from the car *(book, mostly)*: Supplies +1, Morale −2.
  - Commit to a solo overnight section: Stamina −1, Morale +2, Supplies −1.
  - Drive over to see Centralia: Morale −1, and unlocks the Centralia Journal entry.
- **Arcs & themes:** the trail without Katz. What the walk is for.

### Scene 14: E10, Mount Washington
- **Purpose:** the wilderness at its most dangerous, faced alone.
- **Book:** Ch 16–17 (verify).
- **What happens:** the White Mountains, above the treeline. The weather turns on the mountain where so many people have died.
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Go for the summit anyway: Morale +2, Stamina −2.
  - Turn back: Stamina +1, Morale −1.
  - Stay the night in a mountain hut: Supplies −1, Stamina +1.

### Scene 15: E11, The Phone Call
- **Purpose:** the relationship tested at a distance. Third ending check.
- **Book:** Ch 18–19 (verify how the Maine trip is arranged).
- **What happens:** Bryson calls Katz about the Hundred-Mile Wilderness. Katz's reply varies with Bond and with **invited**: if he was invited, he's already been training (badly).
- **Choices → consequences** (Bond can change here although Katz is away):
  - "I miss having you out there.": Bond +2, Morale −1.
  - "I'll sort out the gear and the flights.": Bond +1, Supplies −1.
  - "Only if you feel like it.": Morale +1, Bond −1.
- **After the choice:** clears **Katz away**.
- **Ending check** follows. Bond 2 or less here means Katz says no, which is Broken Partnership.

## Act IV: Maine (late summer)

### Scene 16: Reunion
- **Purpose:** bring Katz back, changed.
- **Book:** Ch 19.
- **What happens:** Katz arrives and his greeting changes with Bond. He admits things have been hard at home (verify the relapse detail). Bryson listens.
- **Choices:** none.

### Scene 17: E12, The River
- **Purpose:** a physical test of the partnership.
- **Book:** Ch 20.
- **What happens:** a fast, cold river has to be forded.
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Ford it together, arms locked: Bond +1, Stamina −1. If Stamina is 3 or less before the choice, also Supplies −1.
  - Camp and wait for the water to drop: Supplies −2, Morale −1.
  - Take the long way upstream: Stamina −1, Miles −.

### Scene 18: E13, Katz Is Lost
- **Purpose:** the climax of Katz's arc and of Bryson's. Fourth ending check.
- **Book:** Ch 20–21.
- **What happens:** Katz falls behind and vanishes. Bryson spends a long, frightened night alone. Katz turns up the next day on a logging road, having thrown away his water.
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - Search off-trail at dusk: Stamina −2, Bond +2.
  - Wait at the agreed spot overnight *(book, roughly; verify)*: Morale −2, Supplies −1.
  - Hike out to a road for help: Bond −1, Morale −1. Sets **left the wilderness**, and the Final Crossroads happens on the roadside.
- **Ending check** follows.

### Scene 19: The Final Crossroads
- **Purpose:** the player says, in their own choice, what success means.
- **Book:** Ch 21.
- **What happens:**
  - On the logging road, Katahdin is still far off and a truck could take them out.
  - The narration sums up the journey, changing with the stats and flags.
  - If **skipped ahead** is set, Katz remembers the rental car.
  - Then he asks, "So what now?"
- **Choices → consequences:**
  - "Summit or nothing": Finished Trail if Stamina 5+, Supplies 3+, Bond 5+ and no flags. Otherwise Changed Route.
  - "Let's go home": Early Departure.
  - "Let's finish it our way" *(book)*: Changed Route.

## The Four Endings

The game checks these in order, and the first that fits wins.

**Each ending is:**
- one short illustrated scene;
- an ending card with miles walked against Bryson & Katz's ~870 of 2,190;
- a Bond rating (Strangers, Hiking Buddies or Trail Family);
- a final Katz line;
- the number of Journal entries found, out of 13.

### Scene 20: Broken Partnership
- **Triggered by:** Bond 2 or less at any check.
- **What happens:**
  - At E11, Katz says no to Maine; at other checks, he leaves at the next road.
  - Bryson walks on alone for a day, finds the woods too quiet, and goes home.
  - Weeks later a package from Des Moines: one Little Debbie, no note.
- **Theme:** *no goal is worth more than the person you take with you.*

### Scene 21: Early Departure
- **Triggered by:** Stamina or Supplies at 0, Morale 2 or less at a check, or "Let's go home".
- **What happens:**
  - A motel, a map on the bed. Bryson looks at the miles left, and Katz points at the miles behind.
  - If Katz is away when it triggers, Bryson makes the call himself and Katz says the same thing over the phone.
- **Theme:** *stopping is not the same as failing.*

### Scene 22: Finished Trail (*what if*)
- **Triggered by:** "Summit or nothing" with Stamina 5+, Supplies 3+, Bond 5+, and neither **skipped ahead** nor **left the wilderness** set.
- **What happens:** Katahdin's summit sign at sunrise. Katz is silent for once. The narration admits this is the version the book doesn't have.
- **Theme:** *preparation, care and stubbornness can carry you all the way.*

### Scene 23: Changed Route (*the book's ending*)
- **Triggered by:** "Our way", trying for the summit without the stats, or any flag set.
- **What happens:** they walk out on the logging road and stop. About 870 miles, 39.5 percent, and it doesn't matter: they did it, together. *"We hiked the Appalachian Trail."* The card is tagged **"The book's ending."**
- **Theme:** *the trail humbles the plan; the honest attempt is the success.*

## Continuity Rules and Callbacks

**Running threads**
- **Little Debbies:** from Katz's arrival, through E2's food fight, to the Broken Partnership package.
- **Bears:**
  - the books in the opening;
  - the unanswered noise in E5;
  - one Katz joke in Maine.
- **Mary Ellen:** E3 only. Katz mentions her once in Act II if you let her tag along.
- **The rental car:** E6. It returns as Katz's joke at the Final Crossroads, and as a dashed line on the travel map.
- **The airport goodbye:** E8's choice decides Katz's first line on the phone in E11.
- **Snow, the river and the lost night:** Bryson's fear grows from fear *of the woods* (Act I) to fear *for Katz* (E13). That is his growth.

**The narrator's voice by act**
- **Prologue / Act I:** gear lists, mileage maths, bears.
- **Act II:** starts noticing the woods and Katz.
- **Act III:** alone; asks what the walk is for.
- **Act IV and endings:** honest and reflective.

**Katz's tone follows Bond:** warm at 7+, neutral at 5–6, sharper at 4 or less. Only the line changes. His drinking is never a punchline.

**The Journal:** the 13 entries in docs/story.md. Each unlocks once. Signs and overlooks unlock most of them; E9's Centralia option unlocks one.
